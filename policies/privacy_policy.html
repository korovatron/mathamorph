Privacy Policy for Mathamorph

Effective date: 5 October 2026

This policy covers the Mathamorph app specifically. It sits alongside, and is the documented exception to, the main Korovatron privacy policy, which covers the rest of korovatron.co.uk.

What We Collect

By default, Mathamorph collects nothing. Your equations and saved snippets are stored only in your browser's local storage on your own device, and the app works fully offline.

Google account data collection is optional and only applies if you choose to sign in with Google (from the app's menu) to sync your snippet library across your own devices. When you sign in using Google OAuth, we receive:

  • Your email address
  • Your Google display name

This data, together with your synced snippet library, is stored in a private, per-account Google Firestore database (hosted in the EU) that only your account can read or write.

Why We Store It

The purposes below apply only when you choose optional Google sign-in:

  • Sync your snippet library across the devices you use to access Mathamorph
  • Identify your account if you request data deletion or support

We do not use your personal data for marketing, profiling, or advertising.

Analytics

We use a privacy-focused analytics tool to measure aggregate website traffic, such as page views, visit counts, and high-level browser/device statistics.

GoatCounter is an open-source, privacy-friendly analytics service. It records page views and basic visit data (such as browser type and country) without using cookies or tracking individuals across sites. GoatCounter's privacy policy is available at goatcounter.com/help/privacy.

We do not track what equations or snippets you type, create, or save.

Authentication

Mathamorph offers optional Google Sign-In for cross-device snippet sync. You can use the app fully without signing in. If you choose to sign in, we do not collect or store passwords - your login credentials are managed securely by Google. Signing out at any time stops further syncing; your local work on that device is unaffected either way.

What We Don't Do

We do not:

  • Sell your personal data
  • Share your personal data with anyone else
  • Display your personal information publicly to other users
  • Use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies
  • Use analytics to profile you through individual equations or snippets

We do use trusted service providers: Google/Firebase (for optional sign-in and cloud sync) and GoatCounter (for open-source, cookie-free page view analytics).

Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and any synced snippet data at any time. Just email us at unimatrix@korovatron.co.uk, and we'll verify your identity using your Google account details before proceeding.

Security

If you choose optional Google Sign-In/cloud sync, your account and synced snippet data are stored in Google Firestore, a secure cloud-hosted NoSQL database. We follow best practices to protect your information, but no system is 100% foolproof. We rely on Google's robust infrastructure and encourage you to sign out when you're done on a shared device.
